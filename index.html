      Other numbers

      How we calculate these numbers
      New leads
      People who enquired in the period, counted once each. If the same email or phone number appears again, it is not counted twice.
      Booked a call
      Calls that were scheduled in the period, whenever the call itself takes place.
      Show-up rate
      Calls that took place in the period where the person attended, divided by calls where they attended or did not. Calls still to come, cancelled calls and calls with no outcome marked are left out. With fewer than 8 calls, treat the rate as a rough guide.
      New policies and sales closed
      Deals moved to Won in the period. Sales closed adds up the dollar value on those deals. If the deals carry no value, it shows “Not tracked” rather than $0.
      Change
      Compared with the period just before it, of the same length. For “This week”, the first days of this week are compared with the same days last week. Show-up rate changes are in percentage points.
      Time to first reply
      The middle value, in minutes, between a lead arriving and our first message to them.
      Cost per lead and per booking
      The weekly ad spend we were given, divided by leads or booked calls. Hidden when no ad spend has been entered.
      Dates
      Days are counted in UTC. A week runs Monday to Sunday. Numbers update every night.
